Invite Roulette

  1. Press NEXT. You meet a new person on a video call.
  2. Say hello. Then invite them. Use only the key words on the card. Build the sentence yourself!
  3. They say yes or no. Listen carefully!
  4. At the end, tell your teacher about them: She likes... He doesn't like...
  5. You can also chat! Ask How are you? or Where are you from?
