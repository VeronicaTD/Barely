so and because

Tap each card. What's the difference?

tap 👆
so
reason 🌧 ➡️ result ☂️
gives a result = поэтому
My grandfather was born in Spain, so he speaks Spanish very well.
tap 👆
because
result ☂️ ⬅️ reason 🌧
says why = потому что
He moved to England because his father got a job in London.
